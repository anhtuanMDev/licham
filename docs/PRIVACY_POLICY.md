# Privacy Policy for Lịch Âm (Lunar Calendar)

**Last Updated:** July 27, 2026

## Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains what information our app collects and how it is used. Our core philosophy is simple: **We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data.**

## 1. Information Collection and Use
**Local Only:** Our application operates entirely locally on your device.
- All reminders, settings, and lunar calendar data you enter are stored securely on your device using on-device storage (MMKV).
- We do not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or submit any personally identifiable information (PII) to use the app.

## 2. Cloud Sync and Data Transmission
This app **does not have a cloud sync feature**. We do not send your calendar data, reminders, or any usage metrics to our servers. Your data stays on your phone.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The free version of the app displays advertisements. The premium version (purchased via an in-app purchase) removes all advertising.

- **Google AdMob** *(free version only)*: Used to display banner advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, location data, and other demographic or interest data to serve targeted or contextual ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's privacy settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising).

- **Crash Reporting**: We may integrate anonymous crash reporting to help identify and fix bugs. These tools collect only anonymous stack traces when the app crashes. They do not have access to your calendar events or personal reminders. No personally identifiable information is collected.

## 4. In-App Purchases
The app offers a one-time **Premium** in-app purchase to permanently remove advertisements. Purchase transactions are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

## 5. Permissions
The app may request the following permissions strictly to provide its core functionality:
- **Notifications:** To alert you about the reminders you have set (e.g., anniversaries, lunar calendar events). You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

## 6. Data Retention
All user data (reminders, settings) is stored on your device and controlled entirely by you. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

## 7. Children's Privacy
This app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

## 9. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
